Fretboard overhang is the distance from the outer strings to the edge of the fretboard. If the overhang is too small, it can cause the strings to roll off the edge of the fretboard while fretting.

This measurement is the overhang at the end of the fretboard towards the bridge (heel). This value is typically larger than the overhang at the nut. This is because there is more "play" towards the heel, increasing the risk of the strings rolling off the edge of the fretboard at this end of the neck, especially when bending strings (which is more common on higher frets).

Note: This value is from the center of the outer strings, not the edge of the strings. This means the actual distance will be slightly smaller on the bass side compared to the treble side of the neck due to the difference in string diameters.
